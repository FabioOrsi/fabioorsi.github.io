Fabio Orsi

3D Tech Artist & Developer

As an Industrial Designer with a foundation in Control & Automation Engineering, I have always worked at the intersections between the creative and technical worlds. Focused on 3D models and coding around them, most of my professional contributions fit the definiton of a Technical Artist.

Beyond manual authoring, I have designed dozens of automated workflows that have scaled the production and management of hundreds of thousands of 3D models for various fields including medical, manufacturing, and product visualization for the Web and XR.


  • 3D Modeling & Texturing
    Creating assets for offline rendering, real-time visualization (Web, XR, Game Engines), and digital manufacturing based on concept art, photo references and technical drawings.
    Blender | Substance Painter | Substance Sampler | GIMP | Inkscape
  • Technical Art
    Developing standalone scripts and API-integrated plugins (e.g. for Blender, Substance Painter, GIMP) to standardize and automate pipelines for 3D modeling, texturing and shader setup.
    Blender | Substance Painter | GIMP | Python | Javascript | Google Chrome
  • Systems Integration
    Designing middleware to connect Enterprise Tools (Odoo, Redmine), Web Services (Google Cloud, AWS, Discord), 3D Suites (Blender, Substance), and Game Engines (Unity, Unreal).
    Python | Javascript | Command Line Interfaces (CLI) | C/C++ | C# | Java
  • XR Development
    Creating XR apps (VR/MR) for Quest and Pico headsets with integrations to cloud-based web services (Google Cloud/Firebase) for database and 3D asset management.
    Unity | Unreal Engine | Meta Quest SDK | Pico SDK | Javascript | C/C++ | C#
  • Product Design
    Engineering and designing physical products at different stages of the development cycle, from prototyping to mass production, utilizing specialized CAD software.
    Autodesk Fusion | Autodesk Inventor | PTC Onshape | PTC Creo
  • Industrial Automation
    Automating digital manufacturing pipelines (CNC machining and 3D printing), including tools for bulk order placement, mesh processing, production nesting, and logistics.
    Python | C/C++ | Command Line Interfaces (CLI) | G-Code | C#
  • 3D Artist / Project Manager / Technical Lead
    Spase Inc. - San Jose, California, United States
    Remote - Full-time - Jun 2019 - Dec 2025
    Authoring photorealistic 3D assets for real-time product visualization in e-commerce platforms (Web and mobile AR) based on reference photos and technical drawings.
    Scoping and quoting projects for new and recurring clients, directing and briefing a team of diverse artists, and performing final quality control and technical adjustments on 3D assets.
    Creating internal tools to standardize and optimize production pipelines (e.g. plugins for Blender, Substance Painter, Chrome, GIMP).
    Developing middleware to connect back-end infrastructure (Google Sheets, Firebase, AWS) to team tools (Discord) and 3D authoring software (Blender, Substance Painter).
  • Production Consultant
    Смоленск мебель - Smolensk, Smolensk, Russia
    On-Site - Freelance - Sep 2018 - Dec 2018
    Deploying an ERP at a large-scale furniture manufacturing facility, integrating custom systems such as CCTV, factory access control, inventory management tools and CNC machines.
    Updating legacy designs and technical drawings, and performing the migration of historical manufacturing data into a modern production pipeline.
    Developing real-time scheduling algorithms to synchronize production cycles with live data from sales, inventory, and logistics.
