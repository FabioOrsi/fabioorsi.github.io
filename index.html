Fabio Orsi

3D Artist & Developer

As an Industrial Designer with a foundation in Control & Automation Engineering, I operate at the intersections of creative and technical development.

Beyond manual authoring, I have designed dozens of automated workflows that assisted in the production of hundreds of thousands of 3D models for various fields including medical, manufacturing, and product visualization for the Web and XR.


  • 3D Modeling & Texturing:
    Creating photorealistic 3D assets derived from physical references and engineering data for offline rendering and real-time visualization.
  • Product Design:
    Engineering and designing products utilizing specialized CAD software for prototyping and mass manufacturing.
  • Technical Art & Automation:
    Coding algorithms to streamline and standardize complex pipelines in 3D authoring software such as Blender and Substance Painter, generally in the form of custom tools and plugins.
  • Systems Integration:
    Developing solutions to integrate Enterprise Systems (CRMs/ERPs) and other web services, such as Odoo, Redmine, Google Cloud, AWS and Discord.
  • Industrial Automation:
    Bridging the gap between enterprise systems, 3D authoring software, and manufacturing by automating end-to-end processes from order placement to CNC machining and 3D printing.
