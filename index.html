Fabio Orsi
3D Tech Artist & Developer
Brazil
As an Industrial Designer with a foundation in Control & Automation Engineering, I have always worked at the intersections between the creative and technical disciplines. Focusing on 3D models and coding around them, most of my professional contributions fit the definition of a Technical Artist.
Beyond manual authoring, I have designed dozens of automated workflows that have scaled the production and management of hundreds of thousands of 3D assets for applications in various fields, including medical, manufacturing, and product visualization for the Web and XR.
