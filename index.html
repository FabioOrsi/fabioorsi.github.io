Fabio Orsi

3D Artist & Developer

As an Industrial Designer with a foundation in Control & Automation Engineering, I have always operated at the intersections of creative and technical development. Focused mainly in 3D and coding, my activities have almost always leaned towards those of a Tech Artist.

Beyond manual authoring, I have designed dozens of automated workflows that assisted in the production and management of hundreds of thousands of 3D models for various fields including medical, manufacturing, and product visualization for the Web and XR.


  • 3D Modeling & Texturing:
    Creating photorealistic 3D assets for offline rendering and real-time visualization (Web, XR and Game Engines) derived from concept art, photo references and technical drawings.
  • Technical Art:
    Developing custom algorithms to automate and standardize complex and repetitive processes in 3D authoring software such as Blender and Substance Painter, through standalone scripts or fully API-integrated plugins, addons and extensions.
  • Systems Integration:
    Designing middleware solutions to connect Enterprise Systems (CRMs/ERPs) and cloud-based web services such as Odoo, Redmine, Google Cloud, AWS, Slack and Discord, integrating teams within and between companies.
  • Industrial Automation:
    Bridging gaps between enterprise systems, 3D/CAD software, and digital manufacturing by automating processes from order placement to production (CNC machining and 3D printing), up to packing and shipping.
  • Product Design:
    Utilizing specialized CAD software to engineer and design physical products at different stages of the product development cycle, from prototyping to mass production, always respecting the different constraints intrinsic to every manufacturing process.
